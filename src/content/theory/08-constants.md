## Constants

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Molar mass of carbon | $M_\text{C} = 12.011~\text{g/mol}$ |
| Molar mass of oxygen | $M_\text{O} = 15.999~\text{g/mol}$ |
| Molar mass of CO₂ | $M_{\text{CO}_2} = M_\text{C} + 2 M_\text{O} \approx 44.01~\text{g/mol}$ |
| Molar mass of dry air | $M_\text{dry air} = 28.97~\text{g/mol}$ |
| Total mass of the atmosphere | $m_\text{atm} = 5.148 \times 10^{21}~\text{g}$ |

The conversion factor between the mass of carbon and the mass of CO₂ is the ratio of their molar masses:

$$
\phi = \frac{M_{\text{CO}_2}}{M_\text{C}} \approx 3.664
$$

The amount of air in the atmosphere is

$$
n_\text{air} = \frac{m_\text{atm}}{M_\text{dry air}} \approx 1.777 \times 10^{20}~\text{mol},
$$

so one ppm of CO₂ corresponds to the carbon mass

$$
\varepsilon = 10^{-6} \cdot n_\text{air} \cdot M_\text{C} \approx 2.134~\frac{\text{PgC}}{\text{ppm}} .
$$
